Pesticides Laws and Rules
Pesticide Registration is done to ensure plant protection and enhance food security and food safety in Pakistan. It also helps to maintain biodiversity and safeguard natural resources. The Department of Plant Protection regulates pesticide registration and import under the provisions of the Agricultural Pesticide Act, 1997 & Rules framed thereunder.
